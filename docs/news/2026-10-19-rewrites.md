## Items for John

1. **A claim dropped from the star-formation post.** The old post said the move from 58.8 to 58.0 percent "is inside the day-to-day noise of a spread this wide", linked to the headline file. Neither the headline file nor the method says so, and the method says the opposite: at 200,000 runs with a fixed seed "the percentages then wobble by about a tenth of a point from seed to seed" (`docs/method.md`, parameter 1), so a move of 0.8 points is not noise. The rewrite drops the sentence and says instead that the move was under one point and why. If John wants a line about noise, the accurate one is that the change is eight times the run-to-run wobble.
2. **Ruled by John 2026-10-19: rule 4.19 wins, and the rewritten opener stays.** Before the ruling: the opener is rewritten, though rule 4.13 says "the opener is not rewritten". The brief from the master session asked for every live post, the opener included, and rule 4.19 (later, and the one that wins on the body's shape) applies to every news post. Flagged so John can say which rule governs openers.
3. **Ruled by John 2026-10-19: the Dyson-sphere paragraph is cut** (with it the check of the first automatic reading, which shared the paragraph), bringing the opener to about 1,500 words. Before the ruling: **the opener ran to about 1,600 words**, against rule 4.13's guide of 800 to 1,200 and rule 4.19's allowance that an opener may run longer. The extra length is plain explanation the old post did not give (what a middle answer below one means beside a 42 percent chance; why the shape for whether life starts was switched; what 39 records were). The cheapest cut, if John wants one, is the paragraph on the two Dyson-sphere papers (about 120 words).

Every fact in the new post links to its source in its own sentence, and the full list is below, linked from a one-line note at the foot of the post. That link answers only once this file is on main, so it is the one link in the posts that could not be opened before the merge.

## Cold-reader check, 2026-10-19

Run by a separate reader that saw only the two post files, once as the skeptical stranger of the Cold Reader Protocol and once as the uninformed reader (John's father).

**Where attention broke, and what was done.**
- The opener's lede read as a table of contents. Rewritten as the question and the silence.
- "0.021 civilisations": a fraction of a civilisation, and how the middle can be below one when the chance of company is 42 percent. Both posts now say what the middle answer means in galaxies, and the opener says why it sits below one.
- "the shape of that one range", "the first pass", "a factor of ten", "1 to 20 what?", "observable universe": each replaced or glossed in place.
- The switch of shape for whether life starts had no reason given; it now has the method's reason in two sentences.
- "the first weekly reading ran the same day" (a Friday) beside "every Saturday": now says it was run by hand before the Saturday schedule began.
- "a check of the first reading that ran on its own": now says who had it checked, and what the 39 were.
- The star-formation title's "allows" made the site sound as if it controlled the stars; retitled.
- "Six times the Sun's mass, or ten stars" stopped the father reader; the mass step is dropped.
- "Each point was weighed and given a recommended ruling" beside "I declined the other changes" read as a contradiction; it now says each point came back with a recommendation to take, take part of, or turn down, and that John accepted every recommendation.
- "The middle rose by a third but the chance barely moved" had no reason; one is given.
- "How to Follow It" read as a tour of the menu; cut to two pointers, the feed and Feedback.

**Left as they are, with the reason.** Gamma-ray, radioactive aluminium, dimethyl sulfide, infrared, Enceladus, Europa and Cheyava Falls are named things a reader can follow from their links or their sentence; glossing each would bury the story. The dated rewrite notes stay, because the rule asks for dated notes as one plain line each and the rewrite should be visible.

**Genre pattern-match.** Both posts show things being cut and declined (the review's other points turned down; claims removed; a famous claim that did not hold up), name outside work at full strength, and say what would change the number. Neither paragraph quoted alone reads as machine-made volume.
